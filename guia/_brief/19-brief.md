# 19. Employment, Leaving the Job, and Work Injuries

Scope: money. This section covers only how money is calculated, what to sign, and how deadlines are counted. The first three items are about what you should receive while still employed, the middle six are about leaving the job, and the last seven are about occupational disease and work injuries — work injury money runs an order of magnitude larger than layoff severance and comes with harder deadlines, while the damage from occupational disease cannot be bought back at all.
### 1. Overtime pay comes in three tiers — 1.5x, 2x, 3x; if it is not paid, complain to labor inspection, and late payment adds another 50% to 100%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; routinely keep your own copy of clock-in records, shift schedules, overtime approval forms, and task assignment messages in work group chats
- In plain terms: weekday overtime is paid at 1.5x your wage, weekend overtime with no compensatory time off arranged is paid at 2x, and working on statutory holidays like New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, and National Day is paid at 3x — and you cannot be fobbed off with comp time instead. Overtime is generally capped at 1 hour per day, at most 3 hours for special reasons, and no more than 36 hours per month. If the money is not paid, go to labor inspection; if the employer still fails to pay after being ordered to pay within a set deadline, it must pay an additional amount equal to half to the full amount owed.
- Benefit: statutory working hours are no more than 8 hours per day and no more than 44 hours per week on average; extended working hours generally may not exceed 1 hour per day, and where extended for special reasons, no more than 3 hours per day and no more than 36 hours per month. Three tiers of overtime pay: where extended working hours are arranged, pay of no less than 150% of the wage; where work is arranged on a rest day and no compensatory time off can be arranged, no less than 200%; where work is arranged on a statutory holiday, no less than 300% — for the statutory holiday tier, the statute contains no option of substituting compensatory time off. Where an employer arranges overtime but does not pay overtime wages, the labor administrative department orders payment within a time limit; if payment is overdue, it orders the employer to pay the worker additional compensation at 50% to 100% of the amount owed (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 劳动法（2018 年第二次修正，第三十六、四十一、四十三、四十四条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html>（国家市场监督管理总局刊登）；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第八十五条第三项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: rest days and statutory holidays are not the same thing: statutory holidays are only the few statutorily designated days each year such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, and National Day; adjacent days strung together through make-up workdays count as rest days at 200%. "Company rules require overtime approval" and "I stayed voluntarily" do not categorically exempt the employer from paying, but they do affect the burden of proof, so keep records of task assignments and approvals. Comprehensive working hours systems and flexible working hours systems may only be implemented with approval from the labor administrative department — it is not something the company decides on its own. For the limitation period for arbitration of disputes, see item 17 in section 8.

### 2. Annual leave is 5, 10, or 15 days based on cumulative years of service; untaken days are converted to money at 300% of daily wages
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; sort out your total years of service across all employers — your social insurance contribution records are the proof
- In plain terms: add up your years of service across all employers. Between 1 and 10 years total gets 5 days of annual leave per year, 10 years gets 10 days, 20 years gets 15 days, and switching jobs does not reset the count. If the employer cannot arrange the leave and you agree to skip it, those days are paid at 3x your daily wage — subtracting the wage you get anyway, that is 2x extra in your pocket. Daily wage is monthly wage divided by 21.75. Once you sign a "voluntary waiver of annual leave" form, all you are left with is normal pay.
- Benefit: annual leave entitlement begins after 12 consecutive months of work; the number of days is set by **cumulative** working years — 5 days for 1 to under 10 years, 10 days for 10 to under 20 years, 15 days for 20 years or more — and statutory holidays and rest days do not count toward annual leave. Where the employer genuinely cannot arrange leave due to work needs and the employee agrees not to take it, the employer "pays annual leave wages at 300% of the employee's daily wage" for each untaken day; that 300% includes the share for normal working time, so the actual extra payment is 200%. Daily wage = the employee's monthly wage ÷ 21.75, with monthly wage calculated as the average monthly wage over the 12 months before payment, excluding overtime pay. New hires in the current year get a pro-rata share based on remaining calendar days at the employer; when leaving the job, the leave is pro-rated for time worked that year and paid out, and days already taken in excess are not clawed back. Where an employer neither arranges leave nor pays, it is ordered to correct within a time limit; if it fails to correct, it must additionally pay compensation equal to the amount of the untaken annual leave wages (nationwide, effective January 1, 2008)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 职工带薪年休假条例（国务院令第 514 号，第二、三、四、五、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm>；人力资源和社会保障部 (2008). 企业职工带薪年休假实施办法（人社部令第 1 号，第三、四、五、十、十一、十二、十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Notes: cumulative working time accumulates across employers — it is not "how long you have been at this one" — so if you have changed jobs, do not calculate based on tenure at your current employer. **Only where the employee "for personal reasons and in writing requests not to take the leave" may the employer pay normal wages only** — so do not sign the "voluntary waiver of annual leave" form. You do not get annual leave for a year in which you took personal leave totaling more than 20 days with no wage deduction, or sick leave exceeding the corresponding number of months (2 months for under 10 years of service, 3 months for 10 to 20 years, 4 months for over 20 years). Home leave, marriage and bereavement leave, maternity leave, and the work-injury suspension-with-pay period do not count toward annual leave.

### 3. Probation periods have statutory caps, pay must be at least 80%, and they can be agreed only once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; before signing, check the contract term against the length of the probation period
- In plain terms: for a contract of 1 year or less, probation is capped at 1 month; 1 to 3 years, capped at 2 months; over 3 years, capped at 6 months. The same company can put you on probation only once — doing it again after you are confirmed on staff, say after a transfer, is illegal. Probation pay may not be below the lowest tier for the same position or 80% of the contractually agreed wage, nor below the local minimum wage. For any stretch beyond the legal limit, the employer must additionally pay compensation based on your post-probation monthly wage.
- Benefit: for contracts of 3 months to under 1 year, the probation period may not exceed 1 month; for 1 to under 3 years, 2 months; for fixed-term contracts of 3 years or more and open-ended contracts, 6 months; contracts tied to completion of a specific task or shorter than 3 months may not include a probation period at all; "the same employer and the same worker may agree on a probation period only once"; where only a probation period is agreed, it is invalid and that period is treated as the labor contract term. Probation wages "may not be lower than 80% of the lowest wage for the same position at the employer or of the wage agreed in the labor contract, and may not be lower than the minimum wage standard where the employer is located." Where an illegally agreed probation period has already been performed, the employer must pay compensation for the portion performed beyond the statutory limit, calculated at the employee's post-probation monthly wage. To terminate during probation, the employer may only rely on the statutory grounds and must state the reasons to the worker (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十九、二十、二十一、八十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: the three most common violations: a 1-year contract with a 3-month probation period; a second probation period after confirmation, for instance on a role change; and "no social insurance during probation" — the social insurance obligation runs from the first day of employment and has nothing to do with probation; for the employer-side obligations, see item 12 in section 12. Being dismissed during probation does not mean you get nothing — check whether the employer is actually invoking a statutory ground; for unlawful termination, calculate 2N per item 6.

### 4. When laid off, first calculate N: one month's wage per full year of service, half a month for under six months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; one calculation takes ten minutes
- In plain terms: layoff severance is one month's wage for each full year worked, a period of six months to under a year counts as a full year, and under six months gets half a month. The monthly wage here is the average over the 12 months before you leave, with bonuses, allowances, and subsidies all included — this is the item companies most often lowball. If your wage is more than 3x the local average monthly wage of the previous year, the rate is capped at 3x, and the years counted are capped at 12.
- Benefit: the severance standard under the labor contract law: "for each full year, one month's wage is paid... for six months or more but less than a year, it is counted as one year; for less than six months, the worker is paid half a month's wage"; where the monthly wage is more than three times the local average monthly wage of employees in the previous year, it is calculated at three times that amount, and the years counted may not exceed twelve
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: monthly wage means the average wage over the twelve months before the labor contract is terminated, including bonuses, allowances, and subsidies — not base pay alone. This is the easiest figure for a company to lowball

### 5. If the company fails to give 30 days' notice, it owes an extra month's wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: if the company lets you go without 30 days' written notice, it must pay one extra month's wage on top of N — this is what people commonly call N+1. When negotiating, list this month separately; do not let the company fold it into N.
- Benefit: the labor contract law provides that where the employer terminates the labor contract under that provision, it must "give the worker thirty days' written notice, or pay one additional month's wage, and may then terminate the labor contract." This extra month is commonly known as payment in lieu of notice — money on top of N
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: so the common outcome of a lawful layoff is N+1. When negotiating, keep N and the 1 as separate line items; do not let the company count payment in lieu of notice inside N

### 6. For unlawful termination, damages are twice the severance standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; arbitration takes several months
- In plain terms: where the company's termination is unlawful, damages are double the severance standard — what people commonly call 2N. Whether a termination is actually unlawful depends on the specifics, so consult legal aid or a lawyer first, then decide whether to negotiate or go to arbitration.
- Benefit: the labor contract law provides that where an employer terminates or ends a labor contract in violation of the law, it "must pay the worker damages at twice the severance standard set out in Article 47 of this law"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: this is what is commonly called 2N. What counts as unlawful termination depends on the specifics; consult legal aid or a lawyer before deciding whether to negotiate or arbitrate — see section 7 for the route

### 7. Do not sign a "resignation for personal reasons" — once signed, your N is gone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; withstand the pressure to sign on the spot
- In plain terms: signing a statement that you "resigned voluntarily for personal reasons" usually wipes out both the severance and the unemployment insurance benefits — a departure you initiated is simply not among the situations that trigger compensation (except where the company is at fault, such as owing wages or failing to pay social insurance). When the company says "sign first so we can process it, the compensation will come separately," demand that the promise be written into the agreement on the spot; if it is not written down, do not sign. Refusing to sign that day has no legal consequence whatsoever.
- Benefit: the labor contract law confines the situations requiring severance to a fixed statutory list; a termination initiated by the worker (unless it is because of statutory fault by the company such as unpaid wages or unpaid social insurance) is not among them. Sign a "voluntary resignation" and you usually lose both the severance and the unemployment insurance benefits
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十八条、第四十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: the standard company line is "sign first so we can process it, the compensation will come separately." Demand it be written into the agreement on the spot, or do not sign. Refusing to sign that day carries no legal consequence — take a night to think it over

### 8. Before leaving, save your payslips, attendance records, labor contract, social insurance records, and chat logs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; half an hour
- In plain terms: once you hand back the computer and the accounts, payslips, attendance records, the contract, social insurance records, and chat logs are gone for good. At arbitration, the wage standard and the facts of overtime still have to be proven with materials you hold yourself. What you are saving is your own employment records — do not walk off with the company's source code or client lists; that is a different matter.
- Benefit: once the computer and accounts are handed back, these materials can no longer be obtained. Arbitration does tilt the burden of proof, but the wage standard and the facts of overtime still depend on your own evidence
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；维权路径见第 7 节
- Notes: what you save is your own employment-relationship material; do not casually take the company's source code, client lists, or technical documentation — that is a different matter, see section 11

### 9. Right after leaving, do two things: register as unemployed to claim unemployment insurance benefits, and review your non-compete agreement
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: immediately after leaving, do two things: register as unemployed and claim unemployment insurance benefits, and read your non-compete agreement carefully. Both have time windows — miss them and you forfeit them. Unemployment insurance benefits are payable only if your employment ended through no volition of your own, which is one more reason not to sign a "voluntary resignation" per the previous item.
- Benefit: how to apply for unemployment insurance benefits and employment subsidies is covered in section 7; the compensation standard for non-compete restrictions and the conditions for release are covered in section 11. Both matters have time windows — drag your feet and they lapse
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；具体依据见第 7 节和第 11 节
- Notes: unemployment insurance benefits are payable only when employment ends through no volition of your own, which is one more reason not to sign a "voluntary resignation" per the item above

### 10. Before taking a job with dust, noise, or chemicals, check whether the hazards are stated in the contract; three occupational health examinations are arranged and paid for by the employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; when signing the contract, read the "occupational disease hazard notification" section line by line, and get a copy of every examination report to keep yourself
- In plain terms: when signing the contract, the employer must truthfully state in the contract what occupational disease hazards the position involves, how to protect against them, and what benefits attach — no concealment, no deception. If they concealed it, you are entitled to refuse the work and the employer cannot dismiss you for that. The three occupational health examinations — before starting, during employment, and on departure — are arranged and paid for by the employer, and the results must be given to you in writing. If no departure examination was done, the employer cannot terminate or end your contract. When leaving, you can obtain a copy of your occupational health surveillance file free of charge — this is the key document for a future occupational disease diagnosis.
- Benefit: the occupational disease prevention law: when concluding a labor contract, the employer must "truthfully inform the worker of the occupational disease hazards that may arise in the course of work and their consequences, the protective measures, and the benefits, state them in the labor contract, and may not conceal or deceive"; where during the term of an existing contract the worker is transferred to hazardous work never disclosed in the contract, the employer must again truthfully inform the worker and negotiate changes to the terms; if the employer violates this, "the worker has the right to refuse to perform work involving occupational disease hazards, and the employer may not terminate the labor contract with the worker on that ground." There are three examinations — before taking the post, during employment, and on departure; "the cost of occupational health examinations is borne by the employer," and the results must be communicated to the worker in writing. The employer may not assign anyone who has not had a pre-placement occupational health examination to work involving occupational disease hazards, may not assign persons with occupational contraindications to contraindicated work, and where health damage related to the occupation is found, must transfer the worker out of the original post and arrange proper placement; **"a worker who has not undergone a pre-departure occupational health examination may not have their labor contract terminated or ended"**. When leaving, you "have the right to obtain a copy of your own occupational health surveillance file; the employer must provide it truthfully and free of charge, and must sign and seal the copy provided" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（2018 年修正，第三十三、三十五、三十六条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html>（北京市人民政府转载）
- Notes: three questions at the interview will tell you roughly how legitimate the employer is: does this position involve occupational disease hazards, will it be written into the contract, and who pays for the pre-placement examination. If they cannot answer, or say "just start working and we'll see," weigh the risk yourself. The departure examination clause is the most easily overlooked protection: without it, the employer cannot terminate or end your labor contract, and that examination is the key document for a future occupational disease diagnosis — the same goes for the file copy, so be sure to get it before you leave. Occupational disease itself is handled as a work injury; for the benefits, see item 12 onward.

### 11. Damage from dust, noise, and chemical toxins is irreversible: the employer must provide protective equipment, and you may refuse unprotected work
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; insist on wearing a particulate respirator, earplugs, and goggles, and refuse unprotected work — the price may be coworkers calling you fussy
- In plain terms: pneumoconiosis, noise-induced hearing loss, and chemical eye burns are all in the statutory occupational disease catalog, and once lungs or hearing are damaged there is no treatment that restores them. You have the right to know what hazards your position involves, to demand that the employer provide protective facilities and personal protective equipment, and to refuse unlawful directives and work without protective measures. If the employer cuts your pay or dismisses you for exercising these rights, those actions are void. For dust, use a particulate respirator — gauze masks and disposable surgical masks do not block it; if the employer does not issue earplugs on a noisy job, buy your own, a few yuan a pair.
- Benefit: as of August 1, 2025 the statutory occupational disease catalog comprises 12 major categories and 135 diseases (including 4 open-ended clauses); first among the categories is occupational pneumoconiosis: silicosis, coal workers' pneumoconiosis, graphite pneumoconiosis, carbon black pneumoconiosis, asbestosis, talc pneumoconiosis, cement pneumoconiosis, mica pneumoconiosis, ceramic workers' pneumoconiosis, aluminum pneumoconiosis, welders' pneumoconiosis, and foundry workers' pneumoconiosis, plus one open clause; occupational ear-nose-throat and oral diseases include noise-induced hearing loss and blast-induced hearing loss; occupational eye diseases include chemical burns of the eye and photoelectric ophthalmia. All of these injuries are irreversible — there is no treatment that restores lungs or hearing. The law gives you four rights you can cite on the spot: to know the occupational disease hazard factors present or possible in the workplace, their harmful consequences, and the protective measures that should be taken; to require the employer to provide protective facilities and personal protective equipment meeting occupational disease prevention requirements and to improve working conditions; to criticize, report, and bring complaints about violations of occupational disease prevention laws and regulations and acts endangering life and health; and "to refuse unlawful directives and forced performance of work without occupational disease protective measures." Moreover, "any reduction of a worker's wages, benefits, or other entitlements, or termination or ending of the labor contract, because the worker lawfully exercises legitimate rights, is void" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委、人力资源社会保障部、国家疾控局、全国总工会 (2024). 职业病分类和目录（2025 年 8 月 1 日起实施）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992843.htm>；新版目录 12 大类 135 种的口径见国家卫生健康委 (2024). 新版目录将职业病调整为 12 大类 135 种. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202412/content_6992838.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（第三十九条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html>（北京市人民政府转载）
- Notes: pick the mask correctly — only a particulate respirator blocks dust; ordinary gauze masks and disposable surgical masks do not. On noisy jobs, if the employer issues no earplugs, buy your own, a few yuan a pair; lost hearing does not come back. "Two years, save up, and leave" does not work with pneumoconiosis: silicosis can manifest and keep progressing years after exposure to dust has ended, which makes the departure examination all the more important (see item 10). The new catalog also adds occupational musculoskeletal diseases (carpal tunnel syndrome, limited to manufacturing workers performing prolonged repetitive wrist work or forceful wrist work) and occupational mental and behavioral disorders (post-traumatic stress disorder, limited to police officers, medical personnel, fire and rescue personnel, and other emergency responders involved in handling sudden incidents). For on-the-spot handling of chemical splashes, see item 20 in section 13.

### 12. If you are hurt at work or hit on the commute, the first thing to do is get a work injury determination — if the employer will not file, file yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: bring three documents and make one trip to the social insurance administrative department: the work injury determination application form, proof of the employment relationship, and the medical diagnosis certificate
- In plain terms: being hurt on the job, or being hit on the way to or from work with the main fault not yours, both count as work injuries. But without a work injury determination, the medical costs, the suspension-with-pay period wages, and the disability benefit all evaporate. The employer is supposed to file within 30 days; for any overrun, the employer pays the work injury benefits for that stretch out of its own pocket. If the employer will not file, you, your family, or the trade union can file yourself with the social insurance administrative department within one year — miss that year and there is no applying at all. If the employer says it is not a work injury, the burden of proof is on the employer.
- Benefit: without the determination, the medical costs, the suspension-with-pay wages, and the disability benefit are all lost; if the employer fails to file within 30 days, the work injury benefit costs for that period are borne by the employer itself
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2010 修订). 工伤保险条例（国务院令第 586 号）第十四条列了七种「应当认定为工伤」，含「（六）在上下班途中，受到非本人主要责任的交通事故或者城市轨道交通、客运轮渡、火车事故伤害的」；第十七条「所在单位应当自事故伤害发生之日或者被诊断、鉴定为职业病之日起30日内，向统筹地区社会保险行政部门提出工伤认定申请」「用人单位未按前款规定提出工伤认定申请的，工伤职工或者其近亲属、工会组织在事故伤害发生之日或者被诊断、鉴定为职业病之日起1年内，可以直接向用人单位所在地统筹地区社会保险行政部门提出工伤认定申请」「用人单位未在本条第一款规定的时限内提交工伤认定申请，在此期间发生符合本条例规定的工伤待遇等有关费用由该用人单位负担」；第十九条「职工或者其近亲属认为是工伤，用人单位不认为是工伤的，由用人单位承担举证责任」；第二十条「自受理工伤认定申请之日起60日内作出工伤认定的决定」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: one year is a hard deadline; past it, no application. The burden of proof lies with the employer, not you, but keep evidence of your own: photos from the day of the incident, coworkers' contact details, medical visit records, attendance records. Article 16 lists three situations that are not recognized — intentional crime, drunkenness or drug use, self-harm or suicide. Article 15 separately provides for "deemed work injuries": death from a sudden illness during working hours at the workplace, or death within 48 hours despite emergency treatment.

### 13. Do not believe "hold on until you reach your desk and it counts as a work injury": for a sudden illness, call 120 first, do not rush to clock in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: pushing through a sudden illness to get to the office earns you no extra money. Deemed work injury status requires that the illness strike during working hours and at the workplace; falling ill on the way and never reaching your post does not count in the first place, and if emergency treatment stretches past 48 hours, it stops counting too. The tens of minutes sacrificed to satisfy those conditions are traded against your own chance of survival.
- Benefit: the clause requires death from a sudden illness "during working hours and at the workplace," or death within 48 hours despite emergency treatment; delaying medical care to make the conditions fit means trading survival odds for a payout that is not even certain
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第十五条第一款第（一）项：「在工作时间和工作岗位，突发疾病死亡或者在48小时之内经抢救无效死亡的」视同工伤；第十四条第（六）项只把上下班途中「受到非本人主要责任的交通事故或者城市轨道交通、客运轮渡、火车事故伤害的」列为工伤，途中突发疾病不在其中. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: two conditions must both hold: during working hours, and at the workplace. It does not apply to someone stricken on the way who never reaches the post; where someone falls ill on the job and is taken home or to hospital and dies within 48 hours despite emergency treatment, it may still apply — what is decisive is where the person was when the illness struck, not where they died. Short videos present this clause as "hold on until you reach the office and there's money" — exactly backwards: what actually changes the outcome is whether those first tens of minutes after onset are spent getting to a hospital. Also, 48 hours is a hard line — treatment stretching beyond 48 hours means it no longer counts.

### 14. If the employer never enrolled you in work injury insurance, the benefits are the same — paid in full by the employer to the same standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: if the employer refuses to acknowledge it, arbitration or even litigation follows — months at minimum
- In plain terms: if the employer never enrolled you in work injury insurance, the work injury benefits still stand, only now paid in full by the employer, item for item and at the same standard, not a cent less. Non-enrollment exposes the employer to extra penalties — an order to make up contributions, a daily surcharge of 0.05% (5/10,000), and on overdue payment a fine of 1 to 3 times the amount owed — it is not a discount on what you are owed. "The company never bought work injury insurance, so there's nothing to pay" is a lie.
- Benefit: the regulations state explicitly: where a worker of a non-enrolled employer suffers a work injury, that employer pays the costs according to the items and standards set out in the regulations — identical to the items and standards for enrolled employers
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第六十二条第二款：「依照本条例规定应当参加工伤保险而未参加工伤保险的用人单位职工发生工伤的，由该用人单位按照本条例规定的工伤保险待遇项目和标准支付费用。」同条第一款：责令限期参加、补缴，「自欠缴之日起，按日加收万分之五的滞纳金；逾期仍不缴纳的，处欠缴数额1倍以上3倍以下的罚款」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: "the company never bought work injury insurance for you, so it can't pay" is the most common lie in these cases. Non-enrollment adds penalties for the employer; it does not reduce what you are owed.

### 15. Once the injury stabilizes, get a labor capacity assessment — the disability grade converts directly into money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: one run through the assessment process
- In plain terms: once the injury has stabilized, get a labor capacity assessment; the grade converts directly into money: the one-time disability benefit is calculated on your own wage — grade 5 gets 18 months, grade 6 gets 16 months, grade 7 gets 13 months, grade 8 gets 11 months, grade 9 gets 9 months, grade 10 gets 7 months. Grades 5 and 6 also keep the employment relationship and receive a monthly disability allowance. Do not sign a "one-time full settlement" agreement before the assessment result is out.
- Benefit: the one-time disability benefit converts by grade into months of the worker's own wage: 18 months for grade 5, 16 for grade 6, 13 for grade 7, 11 for grade 8, 9 for grade 9, 7 for grade 10
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第二十一条「经治疗伤情相对稳定后存在残疾、影响劳动能力的，应当进行劳动能力鉴定」；第二十二条「劳动功能障碍分为十个伤残等级，最重的为一级，最轻的为十级」；第三十六条五、六级待遇（一次性伤残补助金 18/16 个月本人工资，按月伤残津贴为本人工资的 70%/60%）；第三十七条七至十级待遇（13/11/9/7 个月本人工资）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: grades 5 and 6 preserve the employment relationship with a monthly allowance; for grades 7 through 10, when the contract expires or the worker initiates termination, there are in addition a one-time work injury medical subsidy (paid from the fund) and a one-time disability employment subsidy (paid by the employer), with specific standards set by provincial governments. Do not sign a "one-time full settlement" agreement before the assessment result is out.

### 16. Distinguish the three payments for death at work: funeral allowance, dependents' pension, and one-time work death benefit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: handling the paperwork
- In plain terms: a death at work involves three payments — do not negotiate only one of them. The funeral allowance is 6 months of the local average monthly wage of employees in the previous year. The dependents' pension is paid monthly on the deceased's own wage — 40% per month for a spouse, 30% per month for each other relative. The one-time work death benefit is 20 times the previous year's national per capita disposable income of urban residents — one single figure for the whole country, so there is no "our local standard here is lower."
- Benefit: the funeral allowance is 6 months of the average monthly wage of employees in the pooled region for the previous year; the dependents' pension is based on the worker's own wage — 40% per month for a spouse, 30% per month for each other relative; the one-time work death benefit is 20 times the previous year's national per capita disposable income of urban residents, one single nationwide figure, with no regional variation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 工伤保险条例第三十九条：「（一）丧葬补助金为6个月的统筹地区上年度职工月平均工资；（二）供养亲属抚恤金按照职工本人工资的一定比例发给由因工死亡职工生前提供主要生活来源、无劳动能力的亲属。标准为：配偶每月40%，其他亲属每人每月30%，孤寡老人或者孤儿每人每月在上述标准的基础上增加10%……（三）一次性工亡补助金标准为上一年度全国城镇居民人均可支配收入的20倍。」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
