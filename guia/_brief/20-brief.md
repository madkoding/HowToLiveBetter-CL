# 20. How to care for a newborn

Scope: infant mortality and money. This section only covers a few measures with large effect sizes and hard evidence; day-to-day childrearing details are outside the scope of this book. For what to handle before the birth and on the day of discharge from the hospital, see Section 27.
### 1. Have the baby sleep on their back, on a firm surface, in the same room but not the same bed, with no soft objects in the bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a firm mattress costs a few hundred yuan
- In plain terms: put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm surface, in your room but not your bed, and keep pillows, duvets, bed bumpers, stuffed toys and head-shaping pillows out of the bed; an inclined cradle or infant seat is not a place to sleep. In the US alone, about 3,500 babies die each year from sleep-related causes, and this is the measure in this section that reduces risk the most.
- Benefit: in the US, about 3,500 babies die each year from sleep-related causes (sudden infant death syndrome, unexplained death, and accidental suffocation in bed). The American Academy of Pediatrics' safe sleep environment recommendations are "back sleeping position; use a firm, non-inclined sleep surface; room-sharing without bed-sharing; avoid soft bedding and overheating"; other risk-reducing measures include breastfeeding, avoiding exposure to nicotine and alcohol, timely vaccination, and pacifier use
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: "no soft objects in the bed" means no pillows, duvets, bumpers, stuffed toys or head-shaping pillows at all, including nursing pillows that look perfectly safe. An inclined cradle or infant seat is not a place to sleep. This is the measure with the largest effect size in this section, and also the one where you most need to stand up to pressure from older relatives

### 2. Give the first hepatitis B vaccine dose within 24 hours of birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; vaccines in the national immunization program are free
- In plain terms: give the first hepatitis B vaccine dose within 24 hours of birth. About 95% of people infected with hepatitis B in infancy or early childhood develop chronic hepatitis, while most adults who get infected clear it on their own, so the earlier this shot is given, the more it helps. If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the baby also needs hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time.
- Benefit: the World Health Organization: "all infants should receive their first dose of hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth, preferably within 24 hours." The reason is that "approximately 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood develop into chronic hepatitis," while most infections acquired in adulthood clear on their own
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>
- Notes: if the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the newborn also needs hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time; this should be established during prenatal checkups. Screening of the mother herself is covered in Section 1

### 3. Complete all the vaccines in the national immunization program — the full course is free, and if you fall behind you only catch up on the missed doses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; the law states that "vaccination units may not charge any fee for vaccines in the immunization program"; it requires several trips to the vaccination clinic by month of age, and keep the vaccination certificate
- In plain terms: all vaccines in the national immunization program are free, and vaccination units cannot charge any fees; nurseries and schools check the vaccination certificate at enrollment, so any missed doses will definitely be dug up and demanded as catch-up then. If you fall behind, you only make up the doses that were not finished — you do not start the whole course over, and you can continue the same vaccine from a different manufacturer. Eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis, stable asthma, and newborn jaundice are not reasons to skip vaccination.
- Benefit: when children enter nursery or school, childcare institutions and schools must check the vaccination certificate, and if a child has not received the immunization program vaccines as required, they must report it to the vaccination unit and cooperate in urging the guardian to complete catch-up vaccination — a missed dose is not "deal with it later"; it will definitely surface at school enrollment. The current schedule is the 2026 edition: human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine has been added to the national immunization program; the DTaP vaccine start age has been moved from 3 months to 2 months, with 5 doses total — 1 dose each at 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months, and 6 years; children of the appropriate age in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps will also routinely receive Japanese encephalitis vaccine from March 2026. Co-administration is allowed: at present all national immunization program vaccines may be given at the same visit according to the immunization schedule or catch-up principles; two or more injectable vaccines should be given at different sites, and it is "strictly prohibited to mix two or more vaccines in the same syringe for injection"; if not given at the same visit, two or more injectable live attenuated vaccines should be at least 28 days apart, while inactivated vaccines and oral live attenuated vaccines have no interval restriction with other vaccines. Catch-up principles (under 18 years old): catch up as early as possible; "only the incomplete doses need to be made up, there is no need to restart the full course"; the same vaccine from a different manufacturer can be continued (nationwide, 2026 edition schedule)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2019). 疫苗管理法（第四十八、四十九条）. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html>（云南省卫生健康委员会转载）；国家疾病预防控制局、国家卫生健康委 (2026). 《国家免疫规划疫苗儿童免疫程序及说明（2026 年版）》解读问答. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Notes: the most common misconception is "the child's constitution is weak, hold off on vaccines." The official position: being an "allergic constitution," having a family history of allergic disease, a history of food allergy, or a history of drug allergy are not contraindications; children with atopic dermatitis (eczema), hives, allergic rhinitis, or bronchial asthma in a stable condition can be vaccinated normally, including those taking oral anti-allergy medication or inhaled corticosteroids; physiological newborn jaundice and breast-milk jaundice are fine for normal vaccination. The actual contraindications are a prior acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine, or a severe allergy to a known vaccine component. For immunodeficiency, ongoing chemotherapy or radiotherapy, or immunosuppressant use, an individualized assessment is needed — explain the situation at the vaccination clinic first. The first hepatitis B dose is item 2. Self-paid, non-program vaccines are billed separately for the vaccine and the service fee and are unrelated to this item.

### 4. Feed only breast milk for the first 6 months — not even water — then start complementary foods at 6 months while continuing to breastfeed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; it also saves the cost of formula; breastfeeding takes time
- In plain terms: start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth, feed only breast milk for the first 6 months — not even water — start complementary foods at 6 months and keep breastfeeding until age 2 or beyond, and make sure complementary foods include iron-rich animal foods every day. If breast milk is insufficient or breastfeeding is not possible, use formula; the gap this item makes is far smaller than the safe-sleep one.
- Benefit: the World Health Organization recommends "beginning breastfeeding within the first hour after birth," "exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months, meaning no other food or liquids are provided, including water," and "starting safe and adequate complementary foods from 6 months of age, while continuing breastfeeding up to 2 years of age or beyond." The National Health Commission's service guidelines likewise promote exclusive breastfeeding from 0 to 6 months and require that from 6 months of age onward, "daily complementary foods must include iron-rich animal foods"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 婴幼儿营养喂养评估服务指南（试行）（国卫办妇幼函〔2024〕452 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Notes: when breast milk is insufficient or breastfeeding is not possible, use formula and do not feel guilty; the gap in effect size is far smaller than that of the safe-sleep item. From 6 to 8 months, add complementary foods 1 to 2 times a day; from 9 to 12 months, 2 to 3 times; the daily foods should cover at least 4 of the 7 food categories

### 5. Make formula with water at 70 ℃ or hotter, let it cool before feeding, and discard any leftovers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan; a few extra minutes of waiting for it to cool
- In plain terms: formula is not sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii; reported case fatality rates in newborns range from 40% to 80%. First pour the hot water into the bottle, wait until the water has cooled to about 73.8 ℃ before adding the powder — the formula will still be above 70 ℃, which cuts this bacterium to less than one hundred-thousandth of its original amount. Let the prepared formula cool before feeding, throw out whatever is left over; preterm babies, low-birth-weight babies and babies under 2 months of age are at the highest risk.
- Benefit: formula is not sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii; the reported case fatality rate after infection in newborns is 40% to 80%. Experimental research shows that if you first pour hot water into the bottle and wait until the water has cooled to about 73.8 ℃ before adding the powder, the formula temperature remains above 70 ℃ in the vast majority of cases, reducing the bacterium by more than 5 log levels
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- Notes: the 70 ℃ threshold comes from public health guidance; Beary et al.'s original wording is "public health guidelines targeting caregivers of high-risk infants recommend preparing powdered infant formula with water heated to at least 70 ℃ (158 ℉) to inactivate microorganisms". Preterm infants, low-birth-weight infants, and infants under 2 months of age are at the highest risk. Do not use water that has sat in a thermos all day, and do not leave prepared formula standing at room temperature until the next feed

### 6. Do not feed honey to babies under 1 year old
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: no honey for babies under 1 year old, including honey water, honey mixed into complementary foods, and pastries containing honey. Honey may carry the bacteria that cause botulism; an adult's gut flora can hold it down, an infant's cannot. The same applies to home-made pickles and fermented foods.
- Benefit: the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: "do not feed honey to children under 1 year of age," because "honey can contain the bacteria that cause botulism." The adult gut flora can hold down these spores; an infant's cannot
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Notes: this includes honey water, honey mixed into complementary foods, and pastries containing honey. By the same logic, do not give home-made pickles or fermented foods to infants

### 7. Make sure the baby gets the vitamin K shot at birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan to a few dozen yuan; usually included in the hospital delivery
- In plain terms: newborns naturally have very low vitamin K stores, and without supplementation they can bleed, with late-onset cases often showing up as bleeding inside the skull. In places without routine vitamin K shots, the incidence of this bleeding is 478 cases per 100,000 newborns, and prevention is estimated to cut it by more than 78%. Legitimate hospitals in China give the injection routinely; if the birth was at home or in an unregulated facility, confirm it yourself. Oral doses work less well than the shot.
- Benefit: newborns have very low vitamin K stores, and without supplementation vitamin K deficiency bleeding can occur, with late-onset cases often presenting as intracranial hemorrhage. The Belgian consensus recommendation is "a single intramuscular dose of 1 or 2 mg of vitamin K at birth"; in a region without systematic provision (42,000 newborns in Tashkent), the incidence of vitamin K deficiency bleeding was 478 cases per 100,000 population, and the authors estimated that prevention could reduce the incidence by "more than 78.5%"
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>；Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Notes: births in legitimate Chinese hospitals include the injection routinely, so you do not need to worry; those giving birth at home or in unregulated facilities should confirm it themselves. Oral regimens are less effective than the intramuscular shot

### 8. Take any baby under 3 months with a temperature of 38 ℃ straight to the hospital — do not wait at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: a few hundred yuan for registration and tests
- In plain terms: if a baby under 3 months reaches 38 ℃, go straight to the hospital — do not observe at home, and do not give fever medicine first and wait. At this age, even a serious bacterial infection may show up as nothing but a fever, and "seems in decent spirits" is not a reason to wait it out at home; for babies under 8 days old there is not even a dedicated guideline, which is all the more reason to seek care immediately.
- Benefit: the American Academy of Pediatrics has a dedicated guideline for well-appearing full-term infants aged 8 to 60 days, defining fever as "temperature ≥38.0 ℃" and giving management pathways in three bands — 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days, and 29 to 60 days; infants under 8 days of age are not even covered by the guideline, which means they need immediate medical care all the more
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>
- Notes: at this age, a serious bacterial infection may present with nothing but fever, and appearing "in decent spirits" is not grounds for observation at home. Do not give fever medicine yourself and then wait and see

### 9. Never shake a baby, no matter how tired or angry you are
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan
- In plain terms: a baby's head is disproportionately large and the neck muscles are weak; violent shaking causes intracranial and retinal bleeding and can kill or leave lifelong disability. The gentle rocking used to soothe a baby to sleep does not count — what is dangerous is the out-of-control kind of shaking. When you are about to lose it, put the baby back in the crib and walk away for a few minutes; letting him cry for a while is far safer than holding and shaking him.
- Benefit: a baby's head is disproportionately large and the neck muscles are weak; violent shaking causes intracranial and retinal bleeding and can be fatal or leave lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to soothe a baby to sleep is different; what is dangerous is violent, out-of-control shaking
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验与普遍共识，本轮未取得可直接引用的原始文献
- Notes: when you truly feel yourself about to lose control, put the baby back in the crib and walk away for a few minutes; letting him cry for a while is far safer than holding and shaking him. This matters most for those caring for a baby alone and chronically short of sleep; handling emotions is covered in Section 3

### 10. Judge diapers not by brand but by three things: fit, how often they are changed, and whether they have ever been flagged in quality spot checks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: anywhere from two to three hundred yuan to over a thousand yuan per month
- In plain terms: no official body has ever ranked "which brand is best"; the only things you can check are the market regulator's product quality spot-check bulletins and investigations of specific incidents. Expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack first and try it for a week — watch for diaper rash and elastic marks — and stock up only if it fits well; diaper rash comes mainly from infrequent changing and poor breathability, and its link to brand is smaller than you would think.
- Benefit: no official body has published a ranking of "which brand is best," so this book gives no brand recommendations. There are only two kinds of verifiable information: first, the product quality spot-check bulletins of the State Administration for Market Regulation, and second, regulators' investigations of specific incidents. After the "formamide in infant diapers" issue drew attention in June 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation led the formation of a joint investigation team to verify it
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 中国政府网 (2026-06-22). 市场监管总局牵头成立联合调查组核查「婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202606/content_7072931.htm>（该页仅有标题，正文未展示，故本条只引用调查这一事实，不引用任何结论）
- Notes: TODO (to be verified: the conclusions of that investigation and the original text of the market regulator's official bulletin). In practice: expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant; buy a small pack first and try it for a week, watching for diaper rash and elastic marks, and stock up only if it fits well. Diaper rash is mainly caused by infrequent changing and poor breathability, and its relationship with brand is smaller than imagined. How to find spot-check bulletins is covered in Section 5

### 11. Consider big-ticket baby items in the order "borrow, buy second-hand, buy new" — do not buy everything at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: the savings usually run to thousands of yuan
- In plain terms: strollers, cribs, swaddles and toys all have very short usable lives, and the second-hand supply far exceeds demand; going in the order borrow, buy second-hand, buy new usually saves thousands of yuan. Do not buy a car seat or a mattress second-hand — structural damage to a seat after a crash is invisible to the naked eye. Postpartum care centers and early-education classes are large non-essential expenses; apply a 24-hour cooling-off period first.
- Benefit: strollers, cribs, swaddles and toys all have very short usable lives, and second-hand supply far exceeds demand. What genuinely cannot be skipped, and should not be bought second-hand, is the car seat (structural damage after a crash is invisible to the naked eye) and the mattress
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: 作者经验，无直接文献；安全座椅的证据见第 1 节，冲动消费见第 5 节
- Notes: for postpartum care centers, early-education classes, baby swimming pools and the like, this book neither recommends nor rules them out, but they are large non-essential expenses and the 24-hour cooling-off period in Section 5 applies
